## The change

A Doxygen class page regenerated by Doxygen 1.9.8 instead of 1.9.1: new attributes (`lang`,
`id="r_..."`), the search-results `<iframe>` replaced by a `<div>` tree, the inheritance image map
regenerated (28 `<area>`s become 9, re-sorted), `<h3>` template header becomes a
`<div class="compoundTemplParams">`, plus licence and version strings. description.md notes that
the script between `@license` tags parses as one `raw_text` node.

## Mapping

Grouped by region; the counts add up to the totals.

- Image map `<area>`s (414 visible, 180 invisible; `matcher`). The ground truth keeps the seven
  `Iir::PoleFilter` areas (before lines 83-89) as after lines 90-96, re-sorted by name, deletes
  before lines 90-110 and inserts the two new areas (after lines 97-98, `SOSCascade` and
  `BaseClass`). omnidiff pairs the LAST nine before areas (lines 102-110, Butterworth/Chebyshev
  BandStop..HighPass) with the nine after areas (lines 90-98) in order, attribute by attribute
  (207 + 90 on the before side, 46 + 20 for after lines 97-98). Each `<area>` pair is a
  `StructurallyIdenticalSubtrees` root and its contents `StructurallyIdenticalAncestor`: the
  kind-only (shape) tier of the phase-1 hash descent (`solve_hash_descent`, second
  `solve_with_hash_map` call) pairs same-shaped `<area>` elements with no regard to their
  `href`/`alt` values, taking the unmapped after candidate nearest by start byte. With the after
  areas taken, `APTED("fast_fallback")` then deletes the seven PoleFilter areas (161 + 70). So the
  content match (identical `href`, near-identical `alt`) loses to a shape-only hash match, not
  to sibling alignment. Why the PoleFilter areas at lines 83-89 were not the ones paired was not
  traced.
- Swapped identical `<td>` twins (46 visible, 20 invisible; `matcher`, `IdenticalHashOfAncestor`
  / `IdenticalHash`). In the member table, `<td class="memItemLeft" ...>void&#160;</td>` is
  byte-identical on the `reset` and `setup` rows. omnidiff maps before line 116's (reset) to
  after line 106 (setup) and before line 118's to after line 104, crossing the rows the ground
  truth (and the rest of each row) pairs.
- Structurally identical but unrelated elements (22 visible, 10 invisible; `matcher`,
  `StructurallyIdenticalSubtrees` root, `StructurallyIdenticalAncestor` contents, the same
  shape-only hash tier). Before line 225 `<a id="a56d24..."></a>` (an anchor the ground
  truth keeps) is mapped to the new `<div id="SRResults"></div>` on after line 61, `a` updated to
  `div`; the ground truth inserts that div.
- Search-results window (22 visible, 9 invisible; `matcher`, `APTED("fast_fallback")`). The ground
  truth keeps `<div id="MSearchResultsWindow">` (before lines 56-60) and maps the iframe's `id`
  attribute into the new `<div id="MSearchResults">`; omnidiff deletes the start/end tags and the
  attribute, and pairs before line 66 `</div>` with after line 65 instead of their own elements.
- Template header (6 visible, 3 invisible; `matcher`, `APTED("fast_fallback")` and
  `APTED("bottom_up_propagation")`): before lines 127-131 `<h3>template&lt;...</h3>`, whose end tag
  the ground truth updates (`h3` -> `div`), is deleted, and the textblock's `</div>` is mapped to a
  different `</div>`.
- Two invisible `UnresolvedNode` deletes of large `<div>`s (before lines 18 and 74) whose
  children are otherwise mapped (`matcher`).

Reasons for the "with children" mismatches re-read on 2026-10-13 after the test helper began
printing them.

## Verdict

- Primary cause: `matcher`
- Other causes: none
- Where a fix would go: the kind-only tier of `solve_hash_descent`
  (`StructurallyIdenticalSubtrees`), which pairs same-shaped elements (the image-map `<area>`s,
  and `<a>` with `<div>` across tag names) on shape alone and should weigh leaf content;
  `IdenticalHash` twin choice by parent agreement
- Ground truth questionable: no
