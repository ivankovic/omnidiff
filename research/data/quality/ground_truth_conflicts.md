# Ground-truth conflicts

The ground truth in `src/test/data/diffs/` is hand-made, and in places it contradicts itself: two
fixtures answer the same question in opposite ways, or one fixture's mapping and paintings
disagree. An engine or renderer change is graded against both sides at once, so a conflict here
blocks any fix that would have to pick a side. This file tracks every known conflict until the
ground truth settles it.

Rules:

- Only Marko edits the ground truth and decides which side a convention takes. Nothing here is a
  proposal for new ground truth.
- No fix is tuned to a fixture listed here. A fix measured against one of them names it and
  reports both sides' numbers.
- When a conflict is settled, move it to *Resolved* with the date and the fixtures that changed.

Earlier censuses: `convention_violations_2026_09_18.md` (cross-fixture painting conventions) and the
intra-fixture invariant rules in `src/test/helper/human_mapping/invariants.rs`
(`assert_ground_truth_invariants`, the `invariants()` test in every stub).

## Conventions answered both ways

Each blocks a change that would otherwise be general. Numbers are from the 2026-10-13 fix work
(AGENT_LOG.md), measured on main 3f7c2b64 or on the fix's own branch.

### 1. Stray renames: may a fragment of replaced code be relabelled into the new code?

A leaf of a replaced expression, under a parent that is not paired, is either renamed into the new
expression (an `update` that crosses unpaired parents) or deleted and inserted. 74 of 1198 human
`update` entries cross unpaired parents.

- **Kept as renames:** `React` of `React.useContext(...)` -> `useOrdinal`
  (`tsx-keybase-client-change-from-one-import-and-call-to-another`), `extraInterfaces` of
  `extraInterfaces != null && ...` -> `serializable` (`java-defects4j-mockito-17-mocksettingsimpl`),
  `Vec` of `Vec<usize>` -> `SubtreeKey` (`rust-hash-optimization`, painted Update),
  `sliceContains` -> `slices` of `slices.Contains` (`go-user-slices-library`).
- **Deleted and inserted:** `RegularTimePeriod` of `RegularTimePeriod.DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE` -> `zone`
  (`java-defects4j-chart-8-week`), `Context` of `Context.OTHER` ->
  `getContextForNoInOperator(context)` (`java-defects4j-closure-123-codegenerator`),
  `java-defects4j-chart-12-multiplepieplot`, `java-defects4j-cli-2-posixparser`,
  `java-defects4j-jacksoncore-12-*`, `java-defects4j-math-35-elitisticlistpopulation`.
- **Blocks:** `solve_stray_renames` one level up, the only general lever for about 80 visible
  mismatches of fragments paired into unrelated new code. Clamped against it today: mockito-17,
  keybase and swift if-to-guard (+1 each), and the rust-hash-optimization and
  protobuf-this-must-be-autogenerated paintings.
- **Status:** tabled by Marko on 2026-10-13 until this ground truth is fixed.

### 2. A renamed identifier in Minimal: painted whole, or only the part that changed?

Invariant 16 says a Minimal painting leaves the shared part of a renamed name alone. Many
Minimal paintings paint the whole identifier, and one name change is painted both ways:
`IDecisionEngineSpecification` gaining `Download` is one Update in
`csharp-sonarr-sonarr-many-updates-with-one-insert` and an insertion in
`csharp-sonarr-sonarr-use-a-different-class`.
